# Work Platform

A low-fee, semi-public-benefit information platform serving ZZP and small/medium businesses through client maintenance tasks, company cooperation and construction-tool/material trade.

## Language

**Client**:
The person or organization requesting home-maintenance work and comparing the offers for its task.
_Avoid_: Customer, buyer

**Guest client**:
A client whose authority covers an individual task, established through confirmed email access without a registered client account.
_Avoid_: Anonymous owner, unverified poster

**Task owner**:
The client authorized to manage a particular task and view its private rough offers.
_Avoid_: Any holder of the task's public identifier

**Company**:
A participating business, including a ZZP or SME, that offers maintenance services, seeks/provides cooperation or trades construction tools/materials.
_Avoid_: Provider, professional, contractor when referring to the platform participant

**Task**:
A client's request for home-maintenance work, distinct from any resulting construction agreement.
_Avoid_: Job, order, contract

**Task publication**:
The first availability of a client's task to eligible companies, marking the start of its availability period.
_Avoid_: Offer publication, draft creation, email confirmation alone

**Material scope change**:
A change to the requested work that can affect a company's rough offer, such as increasing the request from two doors to five.
_Avoid_: Cosmetic correction, automatic acceptance of a new construction agreement

**Scope revision**:
An identified version of the work requested in a task, providing the context for a company's rough offer.
_Avoid_: Offer revision, new task, retention renewal

**Task expiry**:
The end of a task's three-calendar-month availability period, measured from its first task publication without renewal by later activity.
_Avoid_: Physical purge, last-activity timeout, fixed 90-day period

**Voluntary closure**:
The task owner's decision to stop new offers, task alerts and future client-contact disclosure while retaining existing offers until the original task expiry.
_Avoid_: Work award, completion, task erasure, safety hold

**Reopening**:
Restoring a voluntarily closed task before its original expiry, preserving existing offers while requiring fresh opt-in for client-contact sharing.
_Avoid_: New task, renewed expiry, new publication fee, revival of canceled payments

**Task erasure**:
The removal of ordinary task records, including the brief, photos, rough offers, contacts and associated access rights, distinct from task expiry and separately justified billing records or case evidence.
_Avoid_: Task closure, task expiry, hiding content

**Platform billing record**:
The minimum evidence of a company's platform subscription, invoice, refund or related financial effect, distinct from listing/project content and counterpart construction/trading principal.
_Avoid_: Construction invoice, task archive, client contact snapshot

**Evidence hold**:
The preservation of strictly necessary evidence for a named complaint or legal case, with restricted access and a review/deletion date.
_Avoid_: Blanket task archive, indefinite dispute history, automatic retention of every task

**Rough offer**:
A company's private indicative proposal for a task, subject to further discussion, a possible site visit and a final agreement directly with the client.
_Avoid_: Binding quote, accepted contract, winning bid

**Offer withdrawal**:
A company's final withdrawal of a published rough offer for a task in MVP, ending its participation and callback permission without undoing the completed publication service.
_Avoid_: Refund, temporary pause, new purchase opportunity

**Offer publication**:
The first successful availability of a company's rough offer to the client who owns the task.
_Avoid_: Unqualified publication, contact unlock, lead sale, work award

**Publication fee (legacy)**:
The v0.3 once-per-task offer-publication charge, canceled in v0.4 in favor of free and low-price subscription services only; no separate offer-publication charge remains.
_Avoid_: Commission, contact fee, success fee, reading fee

**Publication payment (legacy)**:
A company's individual payment toward first offer publication for one task, distinct from fulfillment of that service and any construction payment.
_Avoid_: Wallet top-up, subscription payment, construction payment, completed publication

**Publication refund (legacy)**:
The return of a company's publication payment when publication cannot be fulfilled or a task is substantiated as fake, duplicate or materially misrepresented, distinct from the outcome of any construction agreement.
_Avoid_: Wallet credit, guaranteed-work compensation, automatic no-reply refund, construction refund

**Published responder**:
A company whose rough offer is currently published to the client for a particular task.
_Avoid_: Every reader, every registered company, selected winner

**Contact preference**:
The task owner's choice between initiating contact with companies and explicitly permitting callbacks through selected client email/phone fields.
_Avoid_: Automatic contact unlock, implied permission

**Callback permission**:
The task owner's revocable permission for every published responder to access the selected client email/phone fields while the task is open; it cannot recall details already copied.
_Avoid_: Per-company selection, public contact listing, address sharing

**Pilot region**:
The area eligible for client task publication during the initial launch: the whole provinces of Groningen and Fryslân, including Drachten.
_Avoid_: Groningen city only, Drachten as a separate province

**Service radius**:
A company's chosen approximate distance from its base area for matching tasks within the pilot region.
_Avoid_: Driving distance, nationwide coverage

**Task alert**:
An opted-in company's notification of matching client tasks, distinct from a paid subscription and necessary account or payment messages.
_Avoid_: Automatic subscription, unrestricted business outreach, account-security notice

**Information item**:
An individual client task, cooperation listing or trade listing, with its own publisher, visibility and availability.
_Avoid_: Page view, email, automatic entitlement

**Cooperation listing**:
A company's invitation for other companies to collaborate on a defined work package.
_Avoid_: Client task, automatically awarded subcontract, disclosure of the original client project

**Work package**:
A defined part of a company's project for which it seeks a cooperating company, distinct from any final cooperation agreement.
_Avoid_: Original client task, employment contract, automatically shared client record

**Trade listing**:
A company's information item for trading construction tools or materials, distinct from a completed transaction or platform-held payment.
_Avoid_: Client task, construction offer, completed order

**Free plan**:
The company service option limited to viewing at most five information items per month.
_Avoid_: Unlimited browsing, paid subscription, one-time trial

**Paid subscription**:
The company service option with no numerical monthly limit on information received during its active period.
_Avoid_: Alert opt-in, unrestricted personal-data access, guaranteed work

**Information access**:
An authorized disclosure of an information item's details to a company, distinct from a minimal preview or management of its own content.
_Avoid_: Raw HTTP request, automatic prefetch, paid offer publication

**Monthly allowance**:
The free plan's monthly limit of five information items; counting and period details require an explicit policy.
_Avoid_: Five HTTP requests, five items per user or board by assumption, carryover balance

**Plan entitlement**:
A company's current service-access rights under its free plan or paid subscription, still subject to ownership, consent, expiry and safety restrictions.
_Avoid_: Alert consent, legal qualification, all-data access

**Publisher**:
The client or company authorized to create and manage an information item, without gaining authority over another party's source project or private records.
_Avoid_: Every subscriber, original-client representative by assumption
