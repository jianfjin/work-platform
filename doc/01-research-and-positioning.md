# Research and differentiated positioning

**Research date:** 20 October 2026 · **Method:** public first-party pages; no account registration, purchase or competitor contact

## 1. Observed evidence

The following descriptions report the platforms' own public claims. They do not verify service quality, actual response times, customer satisfaction or hidden account-only features.

| Platform/source | Observation | Consequence for this proposal |
|---|---|---|
| [Profiman client homepage](https://profiman.nl/) | Free client requests, manual checks and up to three regional quotations; carpentry is one of multiple trades | Verification, local matching and three quotes are not unique advantages |
| [Profiman professional page](https://profiman.nl/vakman/) | Prepaid credits; no subscription; advertised lead prices from €16 excluding VAT; no more than two other competitors; invalid/duplicate/non-genuine leads can be reported within 48 hours for assessed credit restoration | Do not claim prepaid credits, a competition cap or invalid-lead refunds are new. Compare fee basis carefully: our proposed €1 is VAT-inclusive and buys thread access, not the same product as contact details |
| [Werkspot homepage](https://www.werkspot.nl/) | Clients post jobs, professionals respond, clients compare profiles; the product also promotes app messaging and reviews | Basic marketplace, messaging and reviews are baseline features, not differentiation |
| [Werkspot professional help](https://support.werkspot.nl/s/article/How-do-I-get-in-contact-with-a-consumer) | A professional can express interest; the fee is triggered when a client selects them and shares contact details | Our proposal charges earlier, on an explicit full-thread unlock. Lower price does not automatically make this a better risk allocation |
| [Werkspot lead explanation](https://support.werkspot.nl/s/article/I-wasn-t-selected-for-the-job-So-why-do-I-pay-for-the-lead) | A lead purchase does not guarantee winning; not being selected is not itself a refund reason | Explicitly disclose no guaranteed award and distinguish product failure from losing a fair comparison |

No current exact Werkspot price range is asserted: this research did not obtain an authenticated live fee quote. Profiman pages use varying regional language; do not infer a verified coverage boundary. Competitor comparisons in this pack are limited to these observed attributes.

## 2. Positioning

**For homeowners commissioning small carpentry work and independent local carpenters, HoutMatch is a scope-first job conversation platform. Unlike a generic request-and-contact funnel, its proposed core artifact is a dimensioned, versioned work brief linked to comparable private offers and a transparent €1 access record.**

This describes the intended product emphasis, not proof that competitors lack these features. Validate the combination against hands-on competitor use and interviews before making public superiority claims.

## 3. Candidate advantages and proof required

| Hypothesis | Mechanism we will design | Why it could matter | Experiment / acceptance threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Better brief quality reduces wasted quoting | Category-specific dimensions, annotated photos, material/finish fields, access constraints, explicit unknowns | Carpentry estimates depend on details beyond category and postcode | 10 blinded before/after briefs; median missing-information questions falls by 30% |
| Fixed access economics feel fairer | €1 gross per business/thread; free fit preview; no re-charge on messages or selection; visible ledger | Easier acquisition budgeting | Five usability sessions plus 30 paid users; 80% explain fee correctly; track actual repurchase |
| One clarification improves all quotes | Client publishes a non-personal answer to shared scope Q&A; quotes and commercial messages stay private | Avoid answering the same measurement question three times | At least 25% of multi-provider threads use one shared answer; no material increase in privacy reports |
| Comparable quotes improve selection | Line items, VAT basis, exclusions, lead time, site-visit requirement and validity | Lowest headline price is not always comparable scope | Clients correctly identify two mismatched assumptions in 80% of prototype tests |
| Auditable outcomes improve trust | Record purchase, first reply, client response, resolution and a policy-coded restoration | Users can see what happened without support guesswork | Under 2% billing confusion contacts; all restorations have traceable reasons |
| Craft-specific reputation helps new entrants | Consent-based portfolio, verified business identity, job-linked feedback and “new to platform” label | Avoid presenting zero reviews as incompetence | Interview new businesses; compare response distribution by tenure, without paid ranking |

The first five fit the MVP; richer portfolio provenance and scheduling integrations are later enhancements. Availability in MVP is a self-declared date range, not a synchronized calendar or booking guarantee.

## 4. Parity versus a credible wedge

**Parity:** verified registration, geographic filters, photos, reviews, a limited number of providers, advance fee display, paid credits and invalid-lead support.

**Wedge to test:** carpentry-specific work preparation plus private scope-normalized proposals at a deliberately low, explicit one-time access price, with a measurable client-response restoration policy. No individual feature is claimed to be globally unique.

**Avoid:** “only platform with verified carpenters,” “guaranteed jobs,” “all other platforms oversell leads,” “€1 is cheaper for everyone” or “three competitors” when the intended limit is **three firms total**.

## 5. Discovery script and cold start

Ask clients to show the last carpentry job they tried to commission: missing information, quote differences, privacy preferences and actual selection process. Ask carpenters about their last five inquiries, time-to-quote, travel radius, work value, acceptable acquisition cost and willingness to prepay €10. Collect observed behavior and anonymized examples rather than hypothetical enthusiasm.

Recruit 15–20 verified firms across the first categories before inviting demand. Aim for at least five eligible firms per launch category/service area. Seed 20 legitimate client briefs with explicit consent, not fabricated jobs or scraped contact data. Use supplier referrals and local partnerships before broad paid advertisements.

Concierge assistance during discovery must be separately timed and costed; do not mistake subsidized manual success for scalable automation. Start the paid beta with approximately 100 published eligible jobs or four weeks of activity, whichever is later, within an approved spend cap. Small samples are directional, not statistical proof.

## 6. Research limitations

- Public marketing and help pages are a snapshot, not an exhaustive product audit.
- No original customer interviews, demand estimates or market-size calculation have yet been completed.
- No claim of exclusive technical novelty, conversion lift, profitability or legal clearance is established.
- Pricing and terms can change; recheck sources before publishing external comparisons.
