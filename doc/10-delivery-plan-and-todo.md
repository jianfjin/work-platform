**Version:** 0.4 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026 · **Boundary:** documentation only

The three-board/free-subscription redesign changes the previously approved v0.3 scope. Preserve the selected stack, GDPR requirements and €100/month ex-VAT infrastructure planning budget. Re-estimate after policy/runtime qualification; old 41–61 person-days and 8–10-week examples are archived and do not schedule this scope.

## Budget and estimates
Confirmed infrastructure planning budget: **€100/month excluding VAT** for Workers/Hyperdrive, Render PostgreSQL, Resend, R2/Images, required backups/basic monitoring and necessary staging/test usage. Exact plans and expanded workload capacity remain qualification work; this is not a hard provider billing cap.

Human development/operations, legal/accounting, domains, payment fees/refund reserve, ads and future AI are separate. The expanded design has no revised engineering-day or launch-date commitment. Do not weaken GDPR, retention or recovery requirements to fit an unqualified instance price.

## Done means evidence
Per slice: appropriate policy/real-database/contract/browser evidence, safe DTOs, versioned quota/terms/lifecycle, translated failure states, minimal audit and reproducible migrations/runbooks.
