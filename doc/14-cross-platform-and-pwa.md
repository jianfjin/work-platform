# Web/PWA and device behavior
**Version:** 0.4 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026

Keep one responsive Dutch/English Web/PWA for desktop, Android and iPhone; dedicated native apps remain later scope. The three board routes and Free/Subscription service must work without installation.

## Allowance-safe navigation
Lists return safe previews. Do not consume free information allowance through prefetch, HEAD, link previews, service-worker retries or viewport loading. Use a deliberate detail-access action and the server's current entitlement/grant result. Back/reload/two tabs follow the approved repeat-access and period policy without double-spending.

Quota exhaustion must still allow own-post management, own replies, billing, unsubscribe, support and privacy rights under the proposed scope. A subscription screen cannot silently enable auto-renewal or render an invented price. Show clear pending/expired/changed-plan states after revalidation, not a stale unlimited badge.

## Identity and payments
Cookies need not transfer between browser/installed/bank-return contexts. Recover the existing owned billing operation or task access after appropriate login/link exchange; do not create another purchase from a return URL. Explicit subscription/renewal/method policy needs real device qualification; an earlier €1 checkout test is not sufficient.

## Media and forms
Client tasks accept six optional photos, 10 MB each, JPEG/PNG/WebP/iPhone HEIC to sanitized JPEG/WebP. Show upload and processing states, errors and ordinary file selection when camera permission is denied. Test actual HEIC fixtures plus iPhone picker/camera outputs. New-board media may reuse this pipeline after its scope/retention is configured.

## Offline and updates
Cache only allowlisted public static assets and a non-personal offline page. No private details, work-package content, photos, offers, contacts, grants, billing or trade inquiry in service-worker/local/IndexedDB caches; no offline writes or financial/allowance Background Sync replay. Resume, reconnect, account switch and update revalidate server ownership/plan/expiry/consent before showing protected content.

Preserve unsaved-work/update warnings and accessible standalone back/home navigation, safe areas, keyboard behavior and clear board/audience labels. Do not claim local text is saved if storage/OS eviction can lose it.

## Evidence
Real Android/iPhone ordinary browser and installed flows: board navigation, fifth/sixth detail access, back/prefetch/two tabs, login/task recovery, HEIC, payment return, membership expiry/cancellation, offline, update and logout. Emulated viewports alone do not qualify release; no application/device tests have run.
