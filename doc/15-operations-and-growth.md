# Operations and community growth
**Version:** 0.4 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026

## Mission and roles
Operate a low-fee semi-public-benefit network for ZZP/SMEs with client work, cooperation and tools/materials exchange. Explain actual fees/rights without unverified charity, workmanship, sale or work-award claims. Name human support, finance, privacy/deletion and security owners/backups before live use.

## Three-board operations
| Board | Operating focus |
|---|---|
| Client tasks | Guest ownership, regional fit, client contact consent, fake/misleading task reports and immutable expiry |
| Cooperation | Scope/skills/timing quality, right to share original-project information, confidential parent data and partner conduct |
| Tools/materials | Item/condition/quantity/price accuracy, suspicious or unsafe goods reports, availability and pickup-address privacy |

Use case-scoped evidence and reasoned removal/holds with correction/appeal; no automatic public accusation or invented certification. Define new-board lifecycle/audience/settlement before activation. Direct counterpart agreements are a design proposal and do not remove platform privacy/content duties.

## Free and subscription support
Publish exactly what the five-item monthly service counts. Proposed support tooling shows company/period/item grants and reasoned idempotent corrections, without unrestricted browsing of private content. Subscription status, allowance and alert consent are separate controls.

Operate only free and low-price subscription services; the former €1 offer-publication fee is canceled. Choose subscription price and tax/period/renewal/cancellation/refund terms before activation. Support pending/failed/duplicate renewals, cancellation, downgrade and refund/reversal without claiming access or money has completed before verified facts. Staff refund authority and reserve must match the new approved policy; old per-offer refund triggers do not silently carry over.

## Email and matching
Resend with dedicated subdomain remains selected; exact address/plan/retention and documented US account-data review remain work. Use PostgreSQL outbox/leases and verified event intake, not the old cloud-mail mailbox-user endpoint. Qualify token-safe delivery and provider ambiguity.

Retain recipient-confirmed alert opt-in, daily digest default/optional instant and easy unsubscribe. Board/trade/item-category/area/radius preferences matter. A paying subscriber has no numerical monthly information cap but does not consent to unsolicited marketing. Default safe previews/links; do not deliver full client/work-package/pickup data to bypass plan/consent. Exact free view-versus-receipt counting remains pending.

PDOK remains the location source; minimal server-side queries and internal approximate matching, with terms/CRS/province accuracy qualified. Google Geocoding is unselected comparison history.

## Community growth and funding
Recruit genuine local ZZP/SME participants through partnerships, existing professional networks and consented referrals. Test all expanded trades and actual cooperation/tool-material usefulness. Generic category/region pages may support discovery; private listings/photos/addresses are not SEO stock.

Measure useful private responses/partner interest/inquiries, allowance exhaustion/corrections, paying retention, complaints, actual mail/media/provider/support costs and unsubscribes. Views, subscription payment or a sold marker do not prove completed work or a settled sale.

€100/month excluding VAT remains the infrastructure planning target, not a complete operating budget. Human work, legal/accounting, domains, payment fees/reserve, ads and future AI are separate. Advertising is unapproved separate work; do not reuse old fee-CAC arithmetic as subscription validation.
