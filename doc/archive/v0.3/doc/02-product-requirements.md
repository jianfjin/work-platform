# Product requirements document
**Version:** 0.3 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026 · **Status:** revised design; not implemented

## 1. Authoritative interpretation
Client posting and comparison are **free**. Companies fund the platform. Confirmed pilot: **€1.00 including VAT once per company/task when its first rough offer is successfully published to the client**, collected through individual Mollie-hosted checkout. Reading and edits are free; a client reply is not guaranteed, and failed publication receives a full refund. The fee/collection rules were confirmed on 21 October; Mollie was selected as the sole current provider on 22 October 2026. The confirmed MVP method targets are iDEAL/Wero and cards only, with merchant approval and successful €1 payment, full-refund and phone/PWA return tests required before enabling each method. See [ADR 0001](../docs/adr/0001-charge-for-first-offer-publication.md) and [ADR 0007](../docs/adr/0007-use-mollie-for-current-payment-integration.md). Prepaid bundles, wallets and subscriptions are deferred until after MVP. Actual live activation and operational refund/tax details remain launch work.

The draft's client-€1 alternative is analyzed in [Payments](08-payments-and-economics.md), not enabled. No compulsory €10 wallet, three-company cap, KVK/workmanship certification, in-app contract/award system or construction-payment processing.

## 2. Users, region and scope
- Clients: adults authorized to request work, using secure task-scoped guest ownership in MVP. Optional client accounts, multi-task account dashboards and task linking are deferred until after MVP.
- Companies: registered platform accounts with confirmed email ownership and self-declared business/service/contact details; one owner per company in MVP. This is not a workmanship or credential check.
- Operators: platform administration, content/report handling, platform-fee support, security and deletion; not building-site supervision.

The pilot region is confirmed as the whole provinces of Groningen and Friesland/Fryslân, including Drachten. Task publication validates postcode/city and province membership; company-selected service radii control matches without expanding the pilot region. The region mapping must be validated before launch. Categories include carpentry, windows/doors, non-structural alterations, insulation, internal walls, painting and roof maintenance. No platform-issued engineering/safety advice or certification.

Dutch and English; desktop Web plus Android/iPhone browser and installed PWA. Native apps later.

## 3. Workflow and commercial rules
1. Client supplies short description, postcode/city, email and optional photos, confirmed at up to six per task and 10 MB per photo; budget, timing and measurements are optional. Confirmed rule: the task stays unpublished until email confirmation; short-lived task-scoped email links and fresh-link recovery require no account/password. See [ADR 0002](../docs/adr/0002-confirm-email-for-guest-task-ownership.md).
2. Client reviews exactly what companies can see: city/PC4 area, description and sanitized photos. Full postcode, email, phone and street address are not part of that shared brief.
3. Company signs in, reads suitable open tasks free and drafts an introduction, rough bid/range or “site visit needed,” own website if available, email/phone and availability.
4. The first visible response requires explicit €1 checkout. One response per company/task, versioned edits without a second fee. Duplicate requests/callbacks must not double-charge. Payment failure publishes nothing; payment succeeds after close/delete/material scope change -> no publication and full platform-fee refund.
5. All eligible paid responses appear in the client's private paginated list. No fixed participant limit. Companies see only their own response and cannot retrieve competitor identities, prices, response counts or ordering information.
6. Confirmed default CLIENT_INITIATED: client contacts a company directly. Company contact fields are in its offer; client contact fields are not revealed, even after company payment.
7. Confirmed optional ALLOW_RESPONDERS: an explicit client choice permits every currently eligible published responder to view selected client email/phone fields while the task is open. Explain that twelve published responders means all twelve may access those fields; there is no individual responder selection or fixed three-firm audience. Client can switch back/revoke future disclosure; previously copied details cannot be recalled. See [ADR 0003](../docs/adr/0003-client-controlled-responder-contact.md).
8. Street address/full postcode stays private; sharing it for a site visit is a direct client action outside the brief. No contact details in free description/photos.
9. Client may close at any time. Closing stops new responses, alert sends and future callback disclosure. Existing submitted offers remain client-readable until the deletion deadline unless withdrawn/removed; closure does not prove award/completion or automatically refund a valid earlier response. Confirmed reopening: a voluntarily closed task can reopen before its original expiry with existing offers/fees intact and no new fee for previously published companies. Contact mode resets to private until fresh callback opt-in; canceled attempts never revive. See [ADR 0006](../docs/adr/0006-reopen-without-renewing-fees-or-consent.md).
10. Confirmed: the task's immutable expiry is first task publication to companies plus **three calendar months**; offer publication is a separate event. Edit, close/reopen, new offer or account registration never resets it. Access is denied immediately at the deadline, ordinary live task data/all photo versions are purged within 24h, and isolated backups roll off within 30 days with deletions reapplied before restored service; see [ADR 0004](../docs/adr/0004-expire-tasks-from-first-publication.md). The technical design proposes Europe/Amsterdam calendar arithmetic and UTC storage. Narrow billing/active-company/case-evidence exceptions are confirmed, with exact separate schedules subject to professional review.
11. Companies edit their own active offer without a new fee. Confirmed withdrawal of a published offer is final for that company/task in MVP: client sees the company name and a withdrawn marker, without offer text or company contacts; callback access ends, the original fee remains without automatic refund, and no editing-to-republish, restoration or new checkout is allowed. Fee history remains separate; see [ADR 0006](../docs/adr/0006-reopen-without-renewing-fees-or-consent.md).
12. No mandatory on-platform negotiation, site visit booking, final agreement, construction invoice/payment, performance monitoring or work-success verification.

## 5. States
Task: DRAFT -> EMAIL_PENDING -> OPEN -> CLOSED; safety hold SUSPENDED can restrict it; at delete_at it becomes EXPIRED_FOR_ACCESS -> PURGED. Confirmed CLOSED -> OPEN is available only for voluntary closure before delete_at, preserving offers/fees and the original clock, with no new fee for previously published companies. Reopening leaves contacts private pending fresh opt-in and never revalidates canceled attempts. Suspended tasks need an operator decision; expired/deleted tasks cannot reopen. No old 14-day commercial expiry or recurring three-day reconfirmation rule.

Response: DRAFT -> PAYMENT_PENDING -> PUBLISHED -> WITHDRAWN/REMOVED; purge deletes content. Confirmed WITHDRAWN is terminal for that company/task in MVP: company-name/withdrawn marker only to the client, no callback access, no automatic refund, no restoration/republishing/new checkout even after task reopening. Keep an opaque finance-effect key, not the proposal text, where lawfully required. This differs from an unpublished failed/refunded attempt, after which a deliberate reviewed new attempt can be possible without two retained successful fees.

## 6. Retention contract
Confirmed: at delete_at, all reads, downloads, consent grants and new sends stop immediately. Physical live-store/object-version purge completes within 24 hours; retry/alert on failure. Backups are isolated, inaccessible to ordinary users and age out within 30 days; restore reapplies deletion tombstones before serving. Operational failures do not extend access or silently change these limits.

Confirmed exceptions: minimum platform-fee/invoice records, active company profiles/alert settings and strictly necessary case evidence have separate purpose-specific schedules. Evidence holds require a named case, restricted access and a review/deletion date; accountant/legal review determines exact fields, lawful bases, periods and start dates before launch. Disclose these exceptions; no blanket expired-task archive or retention of task photos/text for analytics. External emails/screenshots/contact copies are outside remote recall.

## 8. Metrics and non-goals
Measure posting success, relevant response rate, fee conversion, client contact clicks, opt-out/complaint rates, refund incidents, deletion lag and cost per eligible job. Label site visits/awards/completion as self-reported if voluntarily supplied. Not required: public reviews, chat, shared Q&A, escrow, subscriptions, full offline use, automatic contractor verification or paid ranking.
