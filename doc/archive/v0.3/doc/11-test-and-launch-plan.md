# Test strategy and release gates
**Version:** 0.3 · **Updated:** 22 October 2026
No application tests have run in this documentation delivery. Cases below are planned implementation evidence.

## 1. Layers
Unit tests for policy/time/normalization; real PostgreSQL integration for fee/publication/closure/refund and uniqueness; contract tests for scoped DTOs; browser E2E and accessibility; real Android/iPhone browser/PWA tests; worker/PSP/backup failure drills. Synthetic data and PSP test mode only before approved production testing.

Incident-refund cases include reports within seven days and credible evidence submitted later, unsupported allegations, no-reply/lost-work-only complaints, and legitimate later scope changes. Only a substantiated qualifying case approves an incident refund. Repeated approval, refund/chargeback overlap and refund after withdrawal must not return more than the €1 principal, erase fulfillment history, restore a withdrawn offer or permit another checkout.

AT-18 also verifies automatic submission for confirmed failed-publication/duplicate-capture cases and the one-business-day submission target measured from verification or staff approval using the documented operating calendar. Cover weekend/calendar boundaries, worker restart, duplicate approval and ambiguous provider timeout without deadline reset or blind resend. Overdue, queued and failed obligations reach escalation; submission attempts/provider acceptance never masquerade as completed refunds or confirmed bank receipt.

For the selected TypeScript/Workers backend, NFR-03 reports a defined route mix, offered/achieved throughput, errors, warm/first-request p50/p95/p99 latency, CPU, database round trips, pool/lock waits and job backlog. Ramp load toward the existing 100-active-user/10,000-task workload; user count is not requests/second. Separate local transaction time, API time and end-to-end PSP/network time. The confirmed PostgreSQL/Cron execution needs failure drills for immediate attempts racing with a scheduled claim, interrupted/overlapping Cron invocations, lease expiry/takeover and stale-owner completion. Sender/domain, actual quota/budget and provider retention/transfer review remain activation evidence; no integration or delivery test has run.

## 4. Adversarial money tests
Use synchronized independent PostgreSQL connections, not sequential mocked promises. Repeat representative races at least 20 times:
- Same company/job attempts and callbacks: unique response/fulfillment, no second retained fee.
- Client close or material edit versus paid verification: valid first publication or full refund obligation, no orphan.
- Close/reopen before a late payment callback: old invalidated capture refunds once despite current OPEN state; existing published companies retain their fee and offers, and client contact stays private until fresh opt-in. Client reopen cannot clear a staff hold or revive expired/deleted content.
- Forged classic callback/body-paid claim or authenticated retrieval with wrong amount/currency/profile/mode/attempt: no unauthorized publication. Unknown valid-format IDs receive generic 200 without arbitrary external fetch; malformed bodies are bounded/rejected.
- Caller or merchant-dashboard method expansion cannot expose methods outside the server's approved iDEAL/Wero/card allowlist. An unsupported-method capture cannot publish and produces a tracked paid-unpublished refund obligation.
- Mollie payment-create timeout: reconcile the stable attempt before another external payment. Repeat after the one-hour idempotency cache expires and after credential rotation; a reused or new key does not make an unresolved retry safe. Recover a callback that arrived before the payment ID was saved via attempt reconciliation.
- `open`, `pending` or `authorized` payment, delayed success/failure, refunded/reversed funds and a forged success URL: no premature publication. Late valid payment after close or stale generation creates one refund obligation.
- Known-ID notification with inbox/outbox commit failure: 503 and no acknowledged lost work. Repeated callbacks after commit may re-verify but produce one business effect. A later refund/chargeback notification with the same payment ID must still run; coalescing preserves a callback arriving during the worker lease. Missed notifications recover through scheduled retrieval.
- Test mode uses real Mollie-hosted checkout and real classic notifications as well as local form-body fixtures. Record test-mode and controlled-live evidence separately; simulated checkout is not proof of an actual bank-app return, and live money waits for the merchant/terms gate.
- Lost refund response, queued refund due to insufficient balance, pending/processing/refunded transitions, canceled/failed refund and later chargeback: reconcile once without duplicate principal payout/reversal. Poll states without classic callbacks; do not treat absence of a callback as failure or completion.
- Crash before/after local commit: rollback everything or recover exactly one effect.
- Deletion while payment pending: no content republished; minimum financial refund continues independently.
- Withdraw/reopen/edit does not enable a re-charge; an explicitly new attempt only follows a definitively failed/refunded unpublished one.
- A published offer withdrawn by its company is terminal in MVP: original fee retained without automatic refund, company-name/withdrawn marker only to client, no callback access, no restoration through edit/new draft/upsert/task reopening/old payment event, and no new checkout. Repeated withdrawal is idempotent; owned receipts remain recoverable with current withdrawn status.

## 5. Cross-platform/security checks
On real phones test ordinary photo selection/camera denial, keyboards/safe areas, cold launch, storage eviction, background/foreground, account switch, deep links, checkout return, offline and waiting-worker updates. Inspect Cache Storage, IndexedDB, local/session storage, history restore, raw HTML/JSON and serialized route data; UI hiding is not authorization.

Prototype at least five users; target 80% comprehension of client free/company €1 event, no guarantee of work, unlimited eligible responder audience, private offers, callback options and three-month deletion/exception wording. Record confusion and amend copy before beta.

## 6. Release checklist
- [ ] Cloudflare qualification passes in the actual chosen runtime: framework/auth, PostgreSQL transactions, no stale authorization/consent/payment reads, durable callback, resumable scheduled jobs and the confirmed Cloudflare Images binding/private-R2 path, including real HEIC, metadata/orientation and deletion during processing. Exercise interrupted/overlapping invocations while preserving the existing fee, refund, consent and expiry assertions; local development alone is insufficient.
- [ ] G0 decisions approved, legal/accountant/merchant/region/contact/retention wording complete in both languages.
- [ ] NFR-09 evidence is complete before any real personal-data use, including alpha: controller/purposes/data flows, reviewed notices/vendor contracts/transfers, DPIA/DPO screening, rights workflow and breach drill; required unresolved safeguards block affected processing.
- [ ] AT-01–AT-27 and critical non-functional evidence pass; no unresolved high-risk finance/privacy/retention bug.
- [ ] Controlled approved live €1 publication and failed-publication refund reconciled with receipts after G2 readiness.
- [ ] Deadline denial, <=24h live purge and <=30-day backup policy proven; restore applies tombstones before exposure.
- [ ] Real Android/iPhone browser and installed PWA checkout/session/update recovery pass.
- [ ] Genuine opt-in local company coverage and client jobs; no fabricated stock or spam.
- [ ] Named support/report/finance/deletion/mail/security owner and backup; incident and refund reserve approved.
- [ ] Beta metrics include sample size, actual costs, refunds and self-reported outcomes honestly.
- [ ] G3 growth requires useful responses and positive actual contribution plus fixed-cost funding, not a promise that €1 must work.

No dedicated apps, new payment accounts, advertising spend or production deployment is authorized by this checklist alone.
