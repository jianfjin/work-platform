# Charge companies for first rough-offer publication

Clients use the platform free; on 21 October 2026 the founder confirmed a €1.00 fee including VAT per company/task for its first successfully published private rough offer, with free reading and edits. Publication is the service the platform can establish while contact and construction agreements remain client-controlled and direct; buying client contact details or charging for completed work would require different permissions or outcome evidence. A client reply or work award is not guaranteed, and a captured payment that cannot result in publication receives a full refund.

The founder subsequently confirmed an individual hosted checkout for each €1 publication fee, deferring prepaid bundles, wallets and subscriptions until after MVP. This avoids upfront commitments and prepaid accounting, accepting one checkout and processing costs on each small payment.

The founder selected Mollie as the sole current payment provider on 22 October 2026 and confirmed iDEAL/Wero and cards as the only MVP method targets; see [ADR 0007](0007-use-mollie-for-current-payment-integration.md). Merchant eligibility and per-method activation evidence remain launch prerequisites.

The founder also confirmed full €1 refunds for substantiated fake, duplicate or materially misrepresented tasks, with a seven-day reporting target from payment and review of credible later evidence. Authorized staff record the approval/reason and necessary case evidence; no reply or losing the work alone remains non-refundable. This exception preserves publication history and does not restore a withdrawn offer or permit another fee. Submission to Mollie is targeted within one business day of verification or staff approval, with automatic submission of verified failed-publication/duplicate-capture cases and escalation of overdue, queued or failed obligations; submission and completion remain distinct. See the [payment policy](../../doc/08-payments-and-economics.md). VAT classification and operational readiness remain launch work; these decisions do not enable live charging.
