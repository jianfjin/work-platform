# Expire task content three calendar months after first task publication

On 21 October 2026 the founder confirmed an immutable task-content expiry three calendar months after the task first becomes visible to companies, with month-end clamping such as 31 January to 30 April. Edits, closing/reopening and new rough offers never extend this deadline, and paid offer publication does not start or reset the clock. This gives clients a predictable availability limit instead of allowing continuing activity to prolong task-data retention indefinitely.

The founder subsequently confirmed immediate access denial at expiry, live task-data erasure including every photo/object version within 24 hours, and isolated backup roll-off within 30 days. Restores must reapply deletions before serving users, preserving a bounded recovery window without allowing recovery to revive expired content; expiry is not a claim that every stored copy is physically erased at that instant.

The founder also confirmed narrow exceptions with separate schedules for minimum platform-fee/invoice records, active company/alert settings and strictly necessary case evidence. Evidence holds require a named case, restricted access and a review/deletion date; they do not authorize a blanket archive of expired tasks. Exact purposes, fields, lawful bases, retention periods and start dates receive accountant/legal review before launch, rather than treating this product decision as legal approval of a particular duration.
