# Reopen tasks without renewing fees, expiry or contact permission

On 21 October 2026 the founder confirmed that a client may reopen a voluntarily closed task before its original expiry, preserving existing offers and paid-fee history without another fee for previously published companies. Reopening retains the original deadline and resets client contact to private until callbacks are explicitly enabled again. Canceled payment attempts remain invalid, so reopening restores task availability without silently restoring an old payment intent or contact permission.

Closure stops new offers, task alerts and callback disclosure; it does not imply construction completion or automatically refund an already published offer. Late captures on unpublished attempts invalidated by closure receive the tracked full-refund treatment, even after reopening. Expired/deleted tasks cannot reopen, and a client cannot use this action to clear an operator safety hold.

The founder also confirmed final withdrawal of a published offer for that company/task in MVP: the client sees the company name and a withdrawn marker, with offer text and company contact details hidden; the company loses callback access and retains the original fee without automatic refund. Neither task reopening, offer editing nor payment retry may republish it or open another checkout. Restoration is deferred, preserving a clear withdrawal action without treating the already completed publication service as a new purchase opportunity.
