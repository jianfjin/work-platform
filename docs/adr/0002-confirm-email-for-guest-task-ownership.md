# Confirm email before publishing guest tasks

On 21 October 2026 the founder confirmed that a client task remains unpublished until the client confirms its email, without requiring registration. Short-lived email links establish access scoped to that task, and a fresh link restores access without requiring an account. This preserves account-free posting while requiring proof of email control before publication, accepting an email-confirmation step in place of immediate publication or compulsory account onboarding.

The founder subsequently confirmed that optional client account creation, a multi-task account dashboard and guest-task linking are deferred until after MVP; company and staff accounts remain in scope. Email recovery supplies the required client management flow without adding a second client identity model for the initial release. Any later linking must prove task ownership and preserve the original expiry; supplying an email address never exposes or claims other tasks.

Exact link/session lifetimes and the authentication provider remain implementation choices.
