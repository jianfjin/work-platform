# Expand the low-fee community platform to three boards and free/subscription access

On 22 October 2026 the founder changed the product to a semi-public-benefit, low-fee platform serving ZZP and SMEs, covering client maintenance tasks, company cooperation/work packages, and construction-tool/material trade. Free companies may view at most five information items per month and active subscribers receive information without a numerical monthly limit; the founder subsequently confirmed cancellation of the former €1 offer-publication fee, leaving only free and low-price subscription services and superseding [ADR 0001](0001-charge-for-first-offer-publication.md). This changes the shared access/billing boundary and delivery scope; exact quota semantics, subscription terms/price and new-board lifecycle/trade-payment rules remain explicitly unresolved in the [v0.4 redesign](../../doc/22-platform-redesign.md), while the selected stack, GDPR requirements and infrastructure planning budget remain in force.
